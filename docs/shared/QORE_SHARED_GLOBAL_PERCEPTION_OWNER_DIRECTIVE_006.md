# QORE Shared — Owner Global Perception Expansion Directive 006

**Status:** OWNER-FROZEN TRANSVERSAL EXTENSION  
**Primary PR:** #635  
**Program:** QORE Meta-Cognitive Scientific Intelligence  
**Source of truth:** GitHub  
**Relationship to Directive 005:** additive; it expands the observation and relational ceiling without deleting, weakening or bypassing any existing Cognitive OS, scientific, causal, sovereignty, anti-leakage or certification law.

## 1. Owner law

```text
SHARED OBSERVATION UNIVERSE
>>
QORE EXECUTION UNIVERSE
```

The current Trader universe must never define the knowledge ceiling of Shared.

Shared must progressively model the global market system available to QORE,
including markets QORE never executes, whenever those markets can supply
causal, relational, contextual, macrostructural, microstructural, regime,
uncertainty or blindspot information.

Observing a market never authorizes trading that market.

## 2. Objective

Shared evolves from understanding isolated markets toward a causal, temporal,
relational and uncertainty-aware model of the market system and the position
of each instrument within that system.

The target observation families include, when available and governable:

- indices and equity breadth;
- FX;
- metals;
- energy and commodities;
- bonds, yields and rates;
- dollar and currency references;
- volatility and variance;
- crypto and alternative risk;
- Asian, European and American markets;
- new provider families not yet represented in QORE.

No family is rejected merely because no current Trader executes it.

uncertainty, regime, persistence, stability, historical validity, current
validity, last transition, provenance and freshness.

## 5. Generalized structural divergence / SMT

SMT is generalized into `CROSS_MARKET_STRUCTURAL_DIVERGENCE`.

No fixed list of ICT-style pairs defines the ceiling. Manually proposed pairs
may seed hypotheses, but Shared must also discover new relationships.

No divergence may be declared from asynchronous or closed markets without
explicit market-hours, liquidity and timestamp comparability.

## 7. Discovery and causal grades

Automatic discovery is required, but every discovered relation remains
epistemically graded:

```text
ASSOCIATION
→ TEMPORAL_DEPENDENCY
→ CAUSAL_CANDIDATE
→ FALSIFICATION_SURVIVED
→ REPLICATED
→ TRANSPORTABLE
→ CERTIFIED
```

No temporal precedence, correlation or information-flow statistic alone may be
promoted to causation.

Discovery must control multiple testing, snooping, selection bias, lookahead,
regime overfit, unstable correlations, provider artifacts, timestamp mismatch,
market-hours mismatch, missing data and asynchronous markets.

## 8. Federation of Worlds

Shared may maintain Equity, FX, Rates, Commodity, Volatility, Crypto,
Liquidity, Broker and Core worlds, but they must exchange evidence and be able
to represent cross-world dependencies without presupposing causality.

Static taxonomy coexists with learned dynamic taxonomy.

## 9. Active Perception

Active Perception must ultimately select not only observation type but also
which market, relationship, horizon and sensor deserves attention now.

It must reason about expected uncertainty reduction, missing information,
redundancy, novelty, relationship instability, prediction error, unexplained
recurrence, data quality, acquisition cost and downstream relevance.

The full observable universe must not be processed at maximum depth on every
runtime decision.

## 10. Market Foundation Model

The QORE Market Foundation Model must be cross-market by construction and may
learn representations of price, time, volatility, liquidity, microstructure,
session, structure, relative movement, cross-asset state, regime, relationship,
temporal dynamics and uncertainty.

Market knowledge remains strictly separated from Trader-specific methodology.

## 11. Multi-horizon law

Relations may coexist at tick/sub-second/seconds/M1/M3/M5/M15/H1/H4/daily/
session/multi-day/weekly/macro horizons.

A relation may diverge locally and remain aligned structurally. Shared must not
collapse those states into one scalar.

## 12. Sensor governance

Every provider market must pass a governed lifecycle:

```text
DISCOVER_PROVIDER_SYMBOL
→ VERIFY_IDENTITY
→ VERIFY_HISTORICAL_AVAILABILITY
→ VERIFY_TIMESTAMP_INTEGRITY
→ VERIFY_MARKET_DATA_QUALITY
→ CLASSIFY_SENSOR_FAMILY
→ MEASURE_REDUNDANCY
→ MEASURE_INFORMATION_GAIN
→ CAUSAL_VALIDATION
→ TEMPORAL_REPLICATION
→ ADMISSION_DECISION
```

Provider availability is discovery, not productive feature admission.

Valid dispositions include `ADMITTED`, `OBSERVE_ONLY`, `REJECTED` and
`QUALIFYING`.

A failed hypothesis does not imply a useless market.

## 13. Scalability law

Global perception must be configuration-driven, schema-driven, adapter-based,
market-agnostic and provider-aware where scientifically valid.

Adding hundreds of observable instruments must not require hundreds of bespoke
implementations.

## 14. Computation and latency

Deep global cognition, discovery and historical science may run outside the
low-latency Trader-facing path and publish causally timestamped, precomputed
states.

The runtime support path remains bounded by QORE latency and availability laws.
Global perception does not authorize a higher runtime SLA.

## 15. Absolute sovereignty

Shared may observe, infer, explain, compare, simulate, remember, hypothesize,
falsify, research and communicate.

It may never BUY, SELL, OPEN, CLOSE, resize, allocate capital, authorize or
reject Risk, modify stop/target, send orders or mutate broker state.

```text
SHARED MUST KNOW MORE
WITHOUT CONTROLLING MORE
```

create a new shortcut work package and does not reduce any existing gate.

- its observation ceiling is not limited by current Traders;
- a governed global sensor registry exists;

- generalized structural divergence and lead/lag are supported as research

- global perception does not leak execution authority.
