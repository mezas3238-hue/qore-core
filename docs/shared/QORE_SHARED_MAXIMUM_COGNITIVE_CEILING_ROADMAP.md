## Mission

Build Shared as the cognitive, scientific and epistemic operating system of QORE Core.

Shared does **not** replace specialist methodology, QORE Risk or Execution. It
observes, reconstructs hidden market state, maintains competing explanations,
simulates plausible worlds, measures uncertainty, diagnoses its own reasoning,
designs controlled research and promotes only validated knowledge.

The final economic examination remains:

- Profit Factor must improve.
- Drawdown must reduce.
- Total R should be preserved or improved.
- Winners must be preserved.
- Avoidable losses should reduce.
- Valid expansions may be extended causally.
- Claims must survive fresh holdout, stress and replication.

## Ordered work chain

### WP-01 — Probabilistic Market Digital Twin

Construct a continuously updated internal twin containing observed state,
latent state, liquidity topology, volatility topology, cross-market
dependencies, regime structure, behavior state, uncertainty and expected state
transitions.

**Exit gate:** point-in-time causal parity, prediction-error ledger, no future
leakage, deterministic replay.

### WP-02 — Federation of Worlds

Maintain competing internal worlds: momentum, liquidity, mean reversion,
inventory/agency, macro/event-dislocation and unknown regime.

Each world is scored by prediction error, causal coherence, calibration,
trajectory accuracy and current evidence.

**Exit gate:** no single-world monopoly; calibrated posterior weights; explicit
model disagreement.

### WP-03 — Causal Discovery Engine

Discover candidate structural relations rather than relying only on
human-authored causal graphs.

Required controls:
temporal precedence, conditional-independence tests, intervention-like natural
experiments where available, cross-regime stability, counterfactual
consistency, replication and falsification.

**Exit gate:** discovered relations remain RESEARCH until replicated and
validated; observational association may never be promoted as proven causality.

### WP-04 — Market Representation Discovery

Learn unnamed latent concepts from market sequences.

The system may create provisional concepts such as `LATENT_CONCEPT_173`, but
must attach provenance, embedding/feature support, episode clusters,
predictive contribution and interpretable probes.

**Exit gate:** concept adds out-of-sample explanatory or predictive information
beyond existing ontology without identity leakage.

### WP-05 — Temporal Hierarchical Brain

Reason jointly across microstructure/ticks, seconds, M1, M3, M5, M15, H1, H4,
daily, weekly and macro regime.

Lower-timeframe opposition must not imply higher-timeframe structural reversal
without fragility/transition evidence.

**Exit gate:** hierarchy reduces false structural-failure declarations while
preserving real terminal detections.

### WP-06 — Market Agency Model

Infer probabilistic behavioral mechanisms such as liquidity seeking, hedging,
forced liquidation, momentum chasing, inventory adjustment, passive
absorption, arbitrage, rebalancing and risk-off transition.

These are latent behavior hypotheses, never claims of known actor identity.

**Exit gate:** agency-state estimates improve scenario discrimination and
calibration out of sample.

### WP-07 — Counterfactual World Engine

Generate alternative worlds under causal perturbations:
liquidity holds/fails, volatility shifts, leader reverses, relationship breaks,
unknown shocks and other bounded state interventions.

Measure path distribution, survivability, tail risk, thesis robustness and
failure probability.

**Exit gate:** no hindsight oracle; all counterfactual decisions respect the
information available at each historical timestamp.

### WP-08 — Epistemic Engine

For every important conclusion record:

- what is believed;
- why it is believed;
- supporting observations;
- contradicting observations;
- assumptions;
- falsification evidence;
- historical familiarity;
- OOD risk;
- causal support;
- aleatoric uncertainty;
- epistemic uncertainty;
- calibration.

**Exit gate:** high epistemic uncertainty lowers cognitive assertiveness;
aleatoric chaos is not mislabeled model ignorance.

### WP-09 — Scientific Society / Ensemble of Minds

Create heterogeneous internal specialists and roles:
Observer, Hypothesis Generator, Causal Scientist, Statistician, Adversarial
Critic, Counterfactual Analyst, Regime Specialist, Trajectory Specialist,
Risk-of-Error Analyst, Replication Scientist plus neural/state-space/graph/
Bayesian/symbolic models where justified.

**Exit gate:** disagreement is represented, provenance preserved, no hidden
majority vote that erases minority falsification evidence.

### WP-10 — Autonomous Scientific Laboratory

Shared detects systematic error patterns, formulates research questions,
creates hypotheses, builds leakage-safe experiments, runs falsification,
replication, holdout and stress, and records conclusions.

Allowed outcomes:
`REJECT`, `RESEARCH_ONLY`, `VALIDATED`, `CERTIFICATION_CANDIDATE`.

**Exit gate:** research cannot mutate runtime or certified knowledge directly.

### WP-11 — Governed Self-Improvement and Continual Learning

Separate:
stable/certified knowledge,
recent validated adaptation,
experimental knowledge.

Require versioning, rollback, provenance, dataset/window identity,
reproduction, catastrophic-forgetting protection and owner-governed
certification.

Promotion chain:

```text
DISCOVERY
→ SANDBOX
→ FALSIFICATION
→ REPLICATION
→ HOLDOUT
→ STRESS
→ SHADOW
→ CERTIFICATION
→ PROMOTION
```

### WP-12 — Cognitive Arbitration + Meta-Cognitive Scientific Intelligence

Build the top-level brain that observes the whole cognitive stack.

It must answer:
- How well am I predicting?
- What am I misunderstanding?
- Where am I overconfident?
- Which world model is failing?
- Which specialist is dominating incorrectly?
- Has the regime changed?
- Which evidence could falsify the current theory?
- Which concepts fail to explain current episodes?
- What should I investigate next?

Cognitive Arbitration communicates Shared Situation to sovereign traders; it
does not seize methodology or execution.

**Exit gate:** self-diagnosis produces measurable out-of-sample improvement
after the full research/governance chain.
