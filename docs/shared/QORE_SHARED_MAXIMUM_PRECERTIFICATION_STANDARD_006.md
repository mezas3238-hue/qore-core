## 1. Absolute law

Shared may not enter its final certification examination merely because CI is
green, individual research work packages are green, VT31 parity exists, or a
subset of cognitive modules has been implemented.

The required sequence is:

```text
BUILD THE MAXIMUM SHARED CEILING
→ CLOSE THE FULL WP-01..WP-12 CHAIN
→ COMPLETE ALL TRANSVERSAL COGNITIVE CAPABILITIES
→ FREEZE SHARED
→ PRE-CERTIFICATION ARCHITECTURE AUDIT
→ ONLY THEN OPEN THE 7-TRADER × 2-YEAR FRESH-HOLDOUT EXAM
→ CERTIFY OR REJECT
```

Until the architecture audit passes and the final exam passes:

```text
SHARED CERTIFICATION STATUS = REJECTED
```

Architecture completion is necessary but not sufficient for certification.
Certification cannot be used as a substitute for missing architecture.

## 2. Identity of the maximum Shared

Shared is the cognitive, scientific and epistemic operating system of QORE
Core.

Its ceiling is not a larger predictor. Its ceiling is a self-observing,
scientifically governed intelligence able to:

- observe market, Core and cognitive reality;
- maintain a probabilistic theory of the current world;
- maintain several competing worlds simultaneously;
- reconstruct hidden state probabilistically;
- discover candidate causal relations;
- discover representations not pre-authored by humans;
- reason across nested temporal scales;
- infer probabilistic market-agency mechanisms;
- predict state transitions and measure prediction error;
- actively request the evidence most useful for discriminating hypotheses;
- represent belief, contradiction, ignorance and uncertainty explicitly;
- simulate causal counterfactual worlds;
- reason with neural, probabilistic, graph, causal and symbolic representations;
- remember episodes, regimes, failures and stable knowledge separately;
- understand market journeys rather than isolated entry snapshots;
- diagnose the stability of market, Core, broker and cognition independently;
- run an internal scientific society with adversarial criticism;
- discover where its own models fail;
- design controlled experiments;
- learn continually without silently overwriting certified knowledge;
- propose self-improvements through a governed promotion chain;
- arbitrate cognition for sovereign QORE consumers without taking their authority;
- know what it knows, what it believes, what it does not know and what would
  falsify its current theory.

The final objective is not:

> predict whether the next candle goes up or down.

It is:

> construct, challenge, falsify and continuously improve the best justified
> model of market and QORE reality available to Core, while preserving explicit
> uncertainty, causal boundaries, provenance and component sovereignty.

## 3. Mandatory maximum capability chain

The existing ordered `WP-01..WP-12` program remains the canonical dependency
chain. The capabilities below are mandatory ceiling requirements and must be
implemented through those work packages and their transversal architecture.

### MC-01 — Probabilistic Market Digital Twin

Shared must maintain a continuously updated market twin containing at least:

- observable state;
- latent state;
- participant/behavior hypotheses;
- liquidity topology;
- volatility topology;
- cross-market dependencies;
- regime structure;
- uncertainty;
- expected state transitions;
- prediction error.

The twin and real market must be compared continuously:

```text
EXPECTED
vs
OBSERVED
→ PREDICTION ERROR
→ MODEL DIAGNOSIS
→ BELIEF UPDATE
```

No inferred state may masquerade as direct observation.

### MC-02 — Federation of Worlds

Shared must not assume a single explanation of the market.

Required world families include, where evidence supports them:

- momentum-dominant;
- liquidity/auction-dominant;
- mean-reversion;
- inventory/agency;
- macro/event-dislocation;
- reflexive/crowding;
- unknown/unexplained regime.

Each world must maintain:

- posterior probability/weight;
- prediction error;
- causal coherence;
- trajectory accuracy;
- calibration;
- supporting evidence;
- contradictory evidence;
- uncertainty.

No single-world monopoly is permitted.

### MC-03 — Dynamic Causal Discovery

Shared must be able to propose candidate relations not explicitly supplied by
human ontology.

Candidate causality must be challenged with:

- temporal precedence;
- conditional-independence controls;
- known-confounder controls;
- intervention-like/natural experiments where available;
- cross-regime stability;
- counterfactual consistency;
- replication;
- falsification.

Observational association never becomes certified causality by naming it so.

### MC-04 — Market Representation and Ontology Discovery

Human concepts are not the ceiling.

Shared must be capable of discovering latent market concepts and provisional
representations, with provenance and interpretable probes.

Each candidate concept must record:

- discovery source;
- supporting sequences/episodes;
- predictive/explanatory contribution;
- relation to existing ontology;
- OOS contribution;
- transportability;
- failure regions.

Ontology operations may include proposal to:

- create;
- split;
- merge;
- refine;
- retire

concepts, but never direct self-promotion into certified runtime knowledge.

### MC-05 — Hierarchical World Model / Temporal Brain

Shared must reason jointly across multiple clocks, including where data exists:


Local pressure may not be equated with higher-timeframe reversal without

### MC-06 — Latent State Reconstruction

Shared must estimate hidden states probabilistically from observable effects,
without claiming unobservable actor identity as fact.

Candidate latent states may include:

- hidden liquidity pressure;
- absorption probability;
- directional pressure;
- inventory imbalance;
- liquidity vulnerability;
- market stress;
- crowding/reflexivity;
- institutional-participation-like evidence only as a probabilistic hypothesis.

Every latent state requires uncertainty and evidence provenance.

### MC-07 — Predictive State Model and Predictive Coding

Shared must predict state transitions, not merely point prices.

Required forms include:

- next-state probabilities;
- multi-horizon transition probabilities;
- regime-transition probability;
- structural-failure probability;
- continuation/expansion probability;
- mean-reversion probability;
- liquidity-sweep/failure probability.

Every important hypothesis should generate expected observations.

Prediction error is first-class cognitive evidence and must modify belief state.

### MC-08 — Active Perception + Value of Information

Shared must be able to ask:

> What observation would best distinguish the currently competing hypotheses?

It must identify missing discriminating evidence and estimate Value of
Information.

Active Perception remains read-only. It may prioritize observation/computation;
it may not mutate trader methodology or broker state.

### MC-09 — Belief State

Shared must maintain explicit distributions over competing explanations.

Beliefs must update sequentially as evidence arrives.

Each material belief must preserve:

- current probability/weight;
- support;
- contradiction;
- assumptions;
- falsification conditions;
- historical familiarity;
- OOD risk;
- model disagreement;
- causal support;
- uncertainty decomposition.

A categorical story without uncertainty is not maximum Shared cognition.

### MC-10 — Market Physics / Constraint Engine

Shared must learn and enforce structural consistency constraints.

"Market physics" is not literal physical law. It means a governed combination
of:

- hard causal/temporal constraints;
- learned transition constraints;
- regime-consistency constraints;
- liquidity/volatility requirements;
- structural acceptance/failure conditions;
- mutually incompatible hypothesis states.

The engine must reject internally inconsistent explanations instead of forcing
them into the belief state.

### MC-11 — Neural-Symbolic Brain

Shared must support a governed combination of:

- deep/self-supervised representation learning;
- probabilistic inference;
- symbolic reasoning;
- causal graphs;
- graph/state-space/sequence models;
- hard market/Core constraints.

Learned representations and symbolic explanations must remain traceable to
evidence and uncertainty.

Static correlation matrices are not the ceiling.

### MC-15 — Uncertainty Decomposition

- model disagreement;
- data uncertainty;

### MC-18 — Counterfactual World Engine

Shared must generate bounded alternative worlds and estimate distributions over
paths rather than deterministic futures.

Examples:

- liquidity holds/fails;
- volatility changes;
- leader reverses;
- cross-market relationship breaks;
- continuation/pullback;
- failed auction/reversal;
- compression persistence;
- unknown shock.

Measure:

- path distribution;
- thesis robustness;
- survivability;
- tail risk;
- failure probability.

No hindsight oracle is allowed.

### MC-19 — Trajectory Intelligence



Journey cognition is required specifically to reduce avoidable losses without
destroying healthy winners.

falsify · research · communicate`

`order_send · enter · exit · close_position · modify_stop · modify_tp ·
position_size · allocate_capital · authorize_risk · block_trade · force_trade`


## 5. Knowledge provenance law

Every promoted knowledge item must answer:

1. What process discovered it?
2. Which code/model/config produced it?
3. Which datasets and exact windows were used?
4. What evidence existed at each causal timestamp?
5. Which competing hypotheses existed?
6. Which falsification tests were run?
7. Which replications succeeded or failed?
8. Which holdout remained untouched?
9. Which stress tests passed?
10. Where does the mechanism fail?
11. What is its knowledge half-life/transportability status?
12. What is its rollback target?

If these cannot be answered, the knowledge is not certifiable.
