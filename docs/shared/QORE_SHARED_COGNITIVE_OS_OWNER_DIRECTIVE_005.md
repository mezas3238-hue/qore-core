## 1. Maximum identity

QORE Shared is the **Cognitive + Scientific + Epistemic Operating System and
intelligence substrate of QORE Core**.

Shared is not a trader and is not defined by Profit Factor, Drawdown or price
prediction. Its highest mission is to maintain the best currently justifiable,
auditable and uncertainty-aware representation of:

- market reality;
- relationships among markets;
- latent states and candidate mechanisms;
- QORE Core reality;
- broker and infrastructure reality;
- cognitive reality across internal agents;
- accumulated knowledge and its decay;
- its own errors and limitations;
- known unknowns;
- and **unknown unknowns: blind areas no QORE component currently knows it is
  failing to observe or represent**.

The governing question is:

> What is the best currently justifiable representation of QORE and market
> reality; what is observed, inferred, hypothesized, contradictory or unknown;
> what could falsify that representation; what evidence is missing; what no
> component knows it is missing; and what should be investigated next?

## 2. Absolute Cognitive Firewall

Shared may:

`observe · infer · explain · compare · simulate · remember · hypothesize ·
falsify · research · communicate`

Shared may never own or acquire:

`order_send · position_close · modify_stop · modify_tp · position_size ·
capital_allocate · risk_authorize · trade_block · trade_force`

Authority is permanently separated:

```text
TRADER     -> setup / methodology decision
CIBO       -> capital allocation and sizing
QORE RISK  -> capital-risk authorization
EXECUTION  -> broker-state mutation
SHARED     -> cognition only
```

No sophisticated future Shared capability may silently bypass this firewall.

## 3. Three simultaneous realities

Shared observes and reconciles three distinct planes.

### Market Reality

Price, ticks, OHLC, volume, spread, depth/order flow when available,
volatility, liquidity proxies, sessions, structure, trajectory, cross-market
relations, rates, indices, FX, commodities, crypto, events, macro state,
regimes and microstructure.

### Core Reality

Feeds, freshness, latency, broker connectivity, process/runtime health, clock
drift, data gaps, symbol mapping, execution quality, spread/slippage/rejection
behavior, Risk state, capital state, telemetry integrity, dependencies, model
versions and failures.

### Cognitive Reality

Shared beliefs, trader observations, CIBO conditions, Risk constraints,
specialist disagreements, experimental knowledge, adaptive knowledge,
certified knowledge, uncertainty, OOD state and explicit unknowns.

Shared must distinguish a market anomaly from a feed, broker, infrastructure or
cognitive-model anomaly.

## 4. Dual digital twins

Shared maintains:

1. a **Probabilistic Market Digital Twin**; and
2. a **QORE Core Digital Twin**.

Every inferred field must preserve:

`value · confidence · evidence · contradictory_evidence · age · source ·
quality`

An inferred state may never masquerade as an observed fact.

## 5. Hierarchical and multiscale cognition

Required temporal levels include:

`tick · sub-second · seconds · M1 · M3 · M5 · M15 · H1 · H4 · D1 · session ·
day · week · macro regime`

Every level owns state, transitions, uncertainty, causal relations, prediction
error and memory. Cross-scale reasoning must support bottom-up, top-down,
delayed and cross-market propagation.

Local adverse pressure is never by itself proof of higher-timeframe structural
failure.

## 6. Federation of Worlds + Unknown World

Shared maintains competing explanatory worlds such as momentum,
liquidity/auction, mean reversion, event/dislocation, inventory,
reflexive/crowding and `UNKNOWN_UNEXPLAINED`.

Worlds compete on explanatory power, prediction error, causal coherence,
calibration, regime compatibility, trajectory accuracy and OOD risk.

Unknown World is first-class. A novel state must not be forced into a known
model merely to avoid abstention.

## 7. Latent state, agency and reflexivity

Shared may infer hidden states and generative mechanisms probabilistically:
absorption, forced flow, crowding, liquidity vulnerability, inventory
imbalance, aggressive/passive pressure, systematic flow, rebalancing,
liquidation, repricing and reflexive feedback.

Unobserved actor identity may never be stated as fact.

## 8. Dynamic causal science

Causal grades are ordered:

`ASSOCIATION → TEMPORAL_DEPENDENCY → CAUSAL_CANDIDATE →
FALSIFICATION_SURVIVED → REPLICATED → TRANSPORTABLE → CERTIFIED`

Every edge must preserve provenance, markets, regimes, dates, sample coverage,
confounders, experiments, failed experiments and transportability limits.

Correlation and temporal precedence may not be promoted directly to causality.

## 9. Predictive coding, negative evidence and active perception

Every important hypothesis must state what should be observed if it is true.
The difference between expected and observed becomes prediction error.

Expected-but-absent evidence is first-class evidence.

Active Perception and Value of Information prioritize observations by their
expected reduction of decision-relevant uncertainty.

## 10. Belief and uncertainty

Shared maintains competing beliefs rather than one mandatory story.

Uncertainty is decomposed into:

`aleatoric · epistemic · model_disagreement · data · regime · causal ·
simulation`

Market randomness and model ignorance must never be conflated.

## 11. Epistemic discipline

Material statements are labeled using states such as:

`OBSERVED · INFERRED · HYPOTHESIZED · SUPPORTED · FALSIFICATION_SURVIVED ·
REPLICATED · CERTIFIED`

Shared must preserve `Known · Likely · Possible · Unknown · Contradictory`
rather than collapsing uncertainty into a binary answer.

## 12. Memory system

Shared requires:

- episodic market memory;
- semantic memory;
- cognitive failure memory;
- provenance memory;
- knowledge half-life;
- mechanism-conditioned transportability;
- invariant discovery;
- analogue retrieval by causal/structural similarity.

Shared must be able to detect recurrence of its own cognitive failure classes.

## 13. Representation discovery and ontology evolution

Human concepts are not the ceiling.

Shared may discover provisional latent concepts and may propose to split,
merge, create or retire ontology concepts when existing categories generate
systematic information loss.

Ontology self-correction remains governed research until validation,
replication, holdout and promotion gates are satisfied.

## 14. Dynamic graph, critical transitions and journey intelligence

Shared models dynamic leader/follower relationships, decoupling, contagion,
correlation breakdown and causal propagation.

It studies critical-transition warnings, market journeys and recovery versus
irreversibility.

Required distinction:

`normal_adversity · recoverable_deterioration · metastable_deterioration ·
critical_transition · structural_failure`

## 15. Counterfactual worlds and simulation fidelity

Counterfactuals are conditional scenario families, not hindsight point
predictions.

Simulation quality must be judged against path distributions, volatility
clustering, tails, transition frequency, regime persistence, cross-market
dependencies and spread dynamics.

## 16. Scientific Society and cognitive arbitration

Required specialists include liquidity, structure, volatility, cross-market,
regime, trajectory, causality, anomaly, agency, macro-time, infrastructure,
broker and epistemics.

Simple voting is forbidden. Arbitration considers calibration, current-regime
competence, OOD, evidence quality, recent prediction error, specialist
relevance and dependency between experts.

Strong hypotheses receive `DEFENDER · PROSECUTOR · SKEPTIC` treatment.

## 17. Self-model, meta-reasoning and computation economy

Shared maintains a model of itself: models relied on, strengths, weaknesses,
stale knowledge, unstable representations and calibration failures.

Meta-reasoning chooses how deeply to think.

Value of Computation asks whether expected information gain justifies the
latency/computation cost.

Runtime cognition is tiered from sensory safety through deep cognition, while
science, discovery and replication remain offline.

## 18. Distributed cognitive attention

Shared cannot process every observable at maximum depth.

Attention is allocated by anomaly, novelty, uncertainty, information value,
system criticality, prediction error, unexplained recurrence and scientific
importance.

This is a cognitive economy, not a fixed polling hierarchy.

## 19. Surprise, novelty and unknown-unknown discovery

Shared distinguishes:

- global anomaly;
- conditional surprise;
- hypothesis-specific surprise;
- cross-market surprise;
- rare-but-known states;
- new combinations of known states;
- genuine OOD;
- possible new regimes;
- data corruption.

The Blindspot system must extend beyond known blindspots and search for
**second-order blindspots**: observation spaces, dependencies or phenomena that
no current QORE component is instrumented or conceptually prepared to ask
about.

## 20. Core, infrastructure and broker intelligence

Market stability and Core stability are separate state variables.

Shared diagnoses feed degradation, clock drift, latency anomalies, mapping
errors, data holes, broker mismatch, spread/slippage/rejection deterioration,
execution degradation and runtime instability.

Broker intelligence maintains independent probabilistic profiles per broker.

Diagnosis never implies mutation authority.

## 22. Sovereign adapters and Blindspot Engine

Generic Shared concepts are translated through sovereign adapters into the
vocabulary of VT08, VT31, Scalper and future components without rewriting
methodology.

Adapters may provide external context but may not inject new hidden setup
rules.

revalidation, never direct rule copying.

The Blindspot Engine records:

- what a component sees;
- what it does not observe;
- what Shared sees outside that field;
- potentially relevant missing information;
- second-order unknown blindspots.

## 23. CIBO and Risk interfaces

Shared may provide CIBO and Risk with contextual state such as stability,
uncertainty, novelty, stress, broker deterioration and system health.

Shared may not allocate capital, size positions, authorize Risk, block trades
or force trades.

## 24. Autonomous Scientific Laboratory

Research loop:

`Observation → Anomaly → Question → Hypothesis → Experiment → Falsification →
Replication → Holdout → Stress → Shadow → Certification → Knowledge Promotion`

Research priority is driven by prediction error, unexplained recurrence,
uncertainty, cognitive failure, knowledge decay, cross-market anomalies and
downstream importance.

Autonomous experiment design is sandboxed.

## 25. Anti-leakage and reproducibility

Every experiment must prove temporal causality boundaries, no future leakage,
no outcome-aware runtime feature construction, holdout isolation and
reproducibility.

Every result binds dataset version, code version, model version, seed,
configuration, provenance and result hash.

## 26. Knowledge governance

Promotion ladder:

`OBSERVATION → HYPOTHESIS → EXPERIMENTAL → FALSIFICATION_SURVIVED →
REPLICATED → HOLDOUT_VALIDATED → STRESS_VALIDATED → SHADOW_VALIDATED →
CERTIFIED`

Knowledge stores remain physically/logically separated:

- `CERTIFIED_KNOWLEDGE`;
- `ADAPTIVE_KNOWLEDGE`;
- `EXPERIMENTAL_KNOWLEDGE`.

No experimental hypothesis may overwrite certified knowledge directly.

## 27. Governed self-improvement and meta-science

Shared may propose changes to models, representations, ontology or research
methodology.

Every self-improvement remains sandboxed, versioned, rollbackable and subject
to falsification, fresh holdout, stress, shadow and owner-governed promotion.

Shared also studies its own scientific process and may propose better
hypothesis-generation or validation methods, but those methods must themselves
be experimentally validated.

## 28. Explanation, provenance and abstention

Every material output must answer:

1. What do you believe?
2. Why?
3. What contradicts it?
4. How certain are you?
5. What would falsify it?
6. What do you not know?
7. What evidence would be most useful next?

Every important belief must trace through hypotheses/models/evidence/sources to
raw observation.

Valid abstention states include:

`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE · UNIDENTIFIABLE · CONTRADICTORY · OOD ·
DATA_UNRELIABLE · NO_SUPPORTED_HYPOTHESIS`

Correct abstention is intelligence.

## 29. Trust, security and degraded mode

Required controls include read-only defaults, signed model artifacts,
immutable provenance, versioned knowledge, role separation, sandbox research,
audit logs and tamper detection.

Partial sensor failure lowers confidence instead of inventing certainty.

If Shared is unavailable, sovereign traders remain conceptually operable.
Shared is augmentation, never an undeclared mandatory methodology dependency.

## 34. Ceiling law

Shared's highest function is not to answer “what will price do?”. It is to
construct, challenge and continuously improve the best justified model of
reality available to QORE while preserving uncertainty and sovereignty.

The final ceiling is reached only when Shared can:

- observe broadly;
- infer without confusing inference with fact;
- preserve competing explanations;
- discover novel representations;
- detect known and unknown blindspots;
- falsify itself;
- correct its ontology;
- remember its cognitive failures;
- preserve and age knowledge;
- conduct reproducible science;
- communicate useful cognition to sovereign components;
- and abstain when evidence is insufficient.

Architecture sophistication alone is not certification.
