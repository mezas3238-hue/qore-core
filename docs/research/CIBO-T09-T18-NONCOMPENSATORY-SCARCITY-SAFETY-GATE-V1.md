`CIBO_T09_T18_NONCOMPENSATORY_SCARCITY_SAFETY_GATE_V1`

## Purpose

The existing frozen T09/T18 scarcity utility evaluator already requires true
scarcity readiness, outcome coverage, realized provider economics, temporal
folds, non-worse settlement drawdown and strict capital-productivity value.

The CE2I closure protocol also states two mandatory scarcity safety dimensions:

- starvation;
- concentration.

Those dimensions were not executable in the existing utility verdict. This
gate closes that Architect-A engineering gap without mutating or weakening the
existing frozen evaluator. **Both** contracts are required when qualifying real
evidence becomes available.

## Causal comparison identity

Every control/treatment fold must preserve the same:

- fresh decision population;
- simultaneous opportunity set;
- Trader strategy/thesis surface;
- provider-economic surface;
- Risk boundary;
- CMA/account capital truth;
- causal horizon;
- protocol binding.

The observation is invalid if the comparison surface drifts.

## Scarcity legality

Every fold must prove:

- true scarcity was actually observed;
- the simultaneous opportunity set was verified;
- the treatment was preregistered before outcomes;
- causal effect was identified;
- provider economics and outcome coverage are complete;
- no future-outcome use;
- no weighted score;
- no productive/certification authority.

T18 additionally requires Trader sovereignty to remain preserved.

## Non-compensatory safety

Treatment may not worsen any fold on:

- maximum drawdown;
- p99 drawdown;
- peak plausible loss;
- peak margin occupancy;
- p95 recovery time;
- provider failures;
- concentration rate;
- starvation rate;
- minimum liquid reserve;
- minimum optionality;
- realized net economic delta.

Higher return cannot rescue starvation or concentration deterioration.

## Strict value

After safety is non-worse, each fold must strictly improve at least one of:

- realized net economic delta;
- capital productivity.

Required replication remains:

`WF1 AND WF2 AND WF3 AND WF4`.

No pooled rescue, 3/4 rescue, weighted rescue or post-outcome winner selection.
