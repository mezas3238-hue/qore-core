# VT08 CRT PURE — OWNER ENGINEERING RESOLUTION DIRECTIVE 001

**Owner directive date:** 2026-09-22  
**Scope:** VT08 CRT PURE — AUDUSD / USDJPY / BTCUSD  
**Status:** ACTIVE RESEARCH GOVERNANCE

## 1. Frozen methodology versus engineering freedom

The existing CRT PURE methodology is the strategic core and is not to be casually
rewritten to chase backtest outcomes.

However, unresolved machine and implementation ambiguities are no longer blocked on a
literal one-to-one closure from RomeoTPT or another original teaching source.

For this project:

- original CRT material remains useful provenance and methodological context;
- QORE Engineering may resolve implementation ambiguities using controlled experiments;
- an engineering rule does not need to be claimed as an original-author rule;
- what matters is that the resulting trader is causal, deterministic, economically useful,
  robust and auditable.

## 2. What remains frozen

The investigation must preserve the current CRT identity at its strategic level:

- scheduled parent CRT construction;
- Turtle-Soup / manipulation-reaction concept;
- lower-timeframe Model #1 confirmation family currently under research;
- structural risk logic;
- causal/no-lookahead execution;
- WAIT / ABSTAIN / hypothesis identity discipline;
- final QORE Risk sovereignty.

A lab may isolate and challenge one of these only through an explicitly new research
identity. It cannot silently rewrite historical evidence.

## 3. Engineering ambiguities may be solved empirically

The following are now explicitly engineering-research questions rather than source blockers:

- same-parent hypothesis competition;
- old-level ownership and mitigation;
- source selection when several valid events coexist;
- rearm versus independent new source event;
- entry-quality gates;
- projected reward/risk acceptance;
- source geometry;
- confirmation latency;
- triplet/session context;
- market-specific context;
- regime/context gating;
- opportunity-density recovery;
- exposure ceilings;
- candidate competition.

The preferred resolver is existing QORE CORE laboratory infrastructure, not unsupported
manual intuition.

## 4. Required experimental discipline

Engineering freedom does not mean unrestricted optimization.

Every proposed rule must be evaluated with as much of the existing CORE research stack as
applicable:

1. causal replay / no-lookahead;
2. hypothesis family frozen before outcome comparison;
3. temporal split / walk-forward;
4. development versus calibration versus external-validation separation;
5. multiplicity control for searched families;
6. performance statistics and drawdown evidence;
7. block-bootstrap / Monte Carlo robustness;
8. start-subwindow stress;
9. cost/slippage perturbation where applicable;
10. parameter-neighborhood robustness;
11. fresh holdout before promotion.

A rule that only improves one consumed window is a research clue, not a promoted edge.

## 5. Market-specific engineering is allowed

AUDUSD, USDJPY and BTCUSD do not have to share every engineering threshold.

A common rule is preferred when evidence supports it, but a market-specific rule is allowed
when:

- the causal mechanism is explicit;
- the rule was frozen before its validation window;
- it survives temporal/OOS evidence for that market;
- it does not exist only to rescue a losing historical slice.

## 6. Density versus edge

Density is an objective, not a quota.

The system may recover valid opportunities hidden by overly restrictive engineering rules,
but it must not manufacture trades by weakening causal validity.

Candidate comparison therefore considers simultaneously:

- trade density;
- profit factor;
- total/mean R;
- drawdown;
- temporal stability;
- OOS stability;
- Monte Carlo / bootstrap behavior;
- stress/slippage robustness.

## 7. Promotion boundary

No laboratory result in the consumed 2Y window alone may authorize:

- DEMO activation;
- LIVE activation;
- production registration;
- real capital;
- merge.

Promotion requires a separately frozen candidate and fresh validation under QORE Trader Lab
governance.
