# CE2I ADR-003 — CIBO Sovereign Capital Intelligence & Compounding

Status: OWNER-DIRECTED / CANONICAL ADDITION TO ADR-002 / RESEARCH ONLY
PR: #651
Date: 29-SEP-2026

## 1. Decision

CIBO evolves from Capital Management Authority into QORE Sovereign Capital Intelligence & Compounding Engine.

ADR-003 does not revoke ADR-002. It extends it.

ADR-002 remains authoritative for Trader/CIBO/Risk/Execution sovereignty and for the current Phase20/21/22 certification lineage.

ADR-003 adds a new research domain:

QORE_COMPOUNDING_INTELLIGENCE_ENGINE
QORE_CORE_COMPOUND_PORTFOLIO

The new domain exists to transform reconciled economic gains into future productive capital while protecting previously conquered capital and preserving Core optionality.

## 2. Constitutional architecture

GLOBAL MARKET -> SHARED -> TRADERS -> CIBO -> RISK -> EXECUTION -> REALIZED ECONOMIC RESULT -> CIBO COMPOUNDING -> FUTURE CAPITAL CAPACITY

Trader owns market methodology.
Shared supplies certified information only.
CIBO owns economic capital decisions.
Risk owns hard survivability constraints.
Execution owns broker mutation.

Binding law:

TRADERS CREATE OPPORTUNITY.
SHARED IMPROVES INFORMATION QUALITY.
CIBO MAXIMIZES ROBUST CAPITAL PRODUCTIVITY.
RISK PREVENTS CAPITAL DESTRUCTION.
EXECUTION MATERIALIZES AUTHORIZED ACTIONS.

## 3. Current V3 non-mutation law

The frozen Phase20 candidate CIBO_PHASE20_FULL_SURFACE_FORWARD_CANDIDATE_V3 must not be retrospectively modified to insert compounding features.

Current Phase20D, Phase21 and Phase22 continue as their existing certification lineage.

Compounding starts in a separate lineage:

CIBO_SOVEREIGN_CAPITAL_COMPOUNDING_RESEARCH_V1

No compounding research artifact may claim to be part of V3 unless a future Owner-directed successor candidate is explicitly created before its own qualifying outcomes.

## 4. Holdout law

CIBO_USD60_6M_HOLDOUT_2017H1_V1 remains SEALED_UNTOUCHED.

It may not be used to design, tune, rank or select compounding policies, floor rules, reserve rules, marginal utility thresholds, crisis behavior, capital generations, portfolio allocation or growth policies.

## 5. Core Compound Portfolio

QORE_CORE_COMPOUND_PORTFOLIO is the account-local economic owner of reconciled compound capital.

It is not a Trader wallet.

A profit may be attributed to VT31, R34, R38, R42, R43, VT08 or another future Trader, but attribution does not create ownership or future allocation priority.

Binding law:

ATTRIBUTION != CAPITAL OWNERSHIP

Once profit is realized, reconciled and admitted under the approved capital policy, it belongs economically to Core inside the account domain where it actually exists.

## 6. Account isolation

Money remains account-local.

CIBO may learn globally, but it may never invent cross-account fungibility.

Each account maintains independent:

- base capital
- realized profit
- protected floor
- reserves
- compound capacity
- deployed compound capital
- risk capacity
- margin capacity
- provider constraints

A global Compound Portfolio view is logical/analytical only unless a real external transfer mechanism exists and is separately governed.

## 7. Compound-capital constitution

Each realized compound dollar must have exactly one current economic state.

Canonical initial state vocabulary:

- PROFIT_PENDING_RECONCILIATION
- REALIZED_PROFIT
- PROTECTED_PROFIT
- COMPOUNDABLE
- STRATEGIC_RESERVE
- OPPORTUNITY_RESERVE
- ACTIVE_COMPOUND_CAPACITY
- DEPLOYED_COMPOUND_CAPITAL
- RELEASED_COMPOUND_CAPITAL
- RETIRED_TO_PROTECTED_FLOOR

These are accounting states, not performance claims.

No dollar may simultaneously be deployable, reserved and retired to a protected floor.

## 8. Floating PnL law

FLOATING PNL != REALIZED CAPITAL.

Floating PnL may inform protection or current risk state but cannot finance compound capacity as cash unless a separately certified mechanism proves and governs that transformation.

## 9. Capital source conservation

Every compound-capital transition must preserve provenance and value conservation.

Permitted value changes require reconciled evidence for:

- realized gain/loss
- execution cost
- provider fee
- settlement
- economic consumption

No synthetic capacity, hidden leverage or double-spend is allowed.

## 10. Capital generations

GEN-0 = original base capital.
GEN-1 = realized profit generated by deployment funded wholly or partly by GEN-0.
GEN-2 = realized profit generated by deployment containing eligible GEN-1 capital.
GEN-N = later descendants with full source lineage.

Generation is provenance only. It does not reduce Risk authority and does not make later capital disposable.

## 11. Protected Capital Floor

CIBO must support a future PROTECTED_CAPITAL_FLOOR_RATCHET.

Protection classification must distinguish:

- ACCOUNTING_PROTECTED
- POLICY_PROTECTED
- BROKER_GUARANTEED

A policy floor is not a broker guarantee.

The optimal ratchet rule is not defined by this ADR and must be separately preregistered and certified.

## 12. Profit preservation and harvesting

Realized profit is not automatically compoundable and compoundable profit is not automatically deploy-now capital.

At least four decisions remain separate:

1. profit reconciliation
2. profit protection / floor ratchet
3. compound eligibility
4. marginal deployment

CIBO must later learn how much to PROTECT, COMPOUND, RESERVE and DEPLOY.

## 13. Compounding speed

Future research may define states such as ACCELERATED_COMPOUND, NORMAL_COMPOUND, CAUTIOUS_COMPOUND, DEFENSIVE, SURVIVAL and COMPOUND_PAUSED.

No numerical thresholds are authorized by this ADR.

Compound speed must eventually depend on calibrated evidence including drawdown, edge quality, uncertainty, provider state, margin, concentration, optionality and Risk headroom.

## 14. Marginal Capital Utility

CIBO must gain a future MARGINAL_CAPITAL_UTILITY_ENGINE.

For each incremental unit of capital it should evaluate at minimum:

- expected incremental return
- incremental stop risk
- incremental margin
- incremental execution cost
- incremental concentration
- incremental drawdown contribution
- incremental optionality consumed
- capital duration / time-to-release
- epistemic uncertainty

The purpose is to answer how much should be deployed, not merely how much can be deployed.

## 15. Shared to CIBO information boundary

A future read-only Shared -> CIBO contract may contain certified facts such as opportunity quality, continuation support, failure hazard, positive-tail potential, regime stability, cross-market coherence, uncertainty, relationship stability and systemic stress.

Binding law:

SHARED INFORMS.
CIBO DECIDES CAPITAL.

Shared gains no sizing, allocation or execution authority.

## 16. Internal Capital Market

T09 and T18 are the current architectural seeds for a future QORE_INTERNAL_CAPITAL_MARKET.

Capital must not be divided equally among Traders.

Future compound allocation may compare opportunities using economic utility, uncertainty, failure hazard, duration, margin, capital velocity, positive-tail potential, concentration, Shared information and execution economics.

## 17. No martingale / no house-money bias

Loss must never automatically increase size.

Realized profit remains real Core capital.

Profit-funded risk is not exempt from survival, drawdown, tail or provider constraints.

## 18. Factor and crisis law

Compounding must not create hidden concentration.

Nominally distinct positions may represent one common risk factor.

Future compound portfolio research must include factor concentration, latent common exposure, tail dependence, correlation convergence, provider degradation, liquidity stress, margin shock and crisis regimes.

Crisis does not imply block everything. The legal response set may include reduce, reserve, minimal seed, selective expansion, aggressive protection or no deployment depending on certified evidence.

## 19. True compounding law

REAL COMPOUNDING means REALIZED ECONOMIC VALUE becomes NEW PRODUCTIVE CAPITAL through reconciled accounting.

Increased leverage without new realized economic capital is not compound growth.

## 20. Certification separation

Compounding requires a separate scientific certification.

Minimum comparison:

CONTROL = exact current CIBO policy that earns certification.
TREATMENT = same opportunities and constraints plus Compounding Intelligence and Core Compound Portfolio.

The treatment may change only CIBO-owned capital actions.

It may not change Trader methodology or Risk hard constraints.

## 21. Compounding certification dimensions

At minimum evaluate:

- ending realized capital
- robust growth rate
- max settlement-cash drawdown
- time underwater
- recovery duration
- profit retention
- protected-floor growth
- capital productivity
- capital-risk-time productivity
- optionality preservation
- tail loss
- declared-model risk of ruin
- multi-regime stability

Higher ending capital does not compensate for an unacceptable survival, drawdown or tail-risk breach.

## 22. Stress requirements

Future certification must include loss clusters, opportunity-arrival variation, correlation convergence, spread/slippage/commission stress, margin shock, capital-release timing stress, simultaneous Trader losses, scarce opportunities, excessive simultaneous opportunities and rapid regime reversals.

## 23. Governance of research

All new compound policy work follows:

IDEA -> SANDBOX -> PREREGISTRATION -> OOS -> STRESS -> REPLICATION -> OWNER/GOVERNANCE -> PROMOTION

No automatic production promotion is allowed.

## 24. First engineering boundary

The first implementation is accounting and provenance, not a compounding formula.

GEN-C1 must create account-local compound-capital lots, generations and durable portfolio conservation with zero runtime authority.

Only after those invariants are green may later generations research protected-floor policies, marginal utility, compound speed, robust growth or crisis behavior.

## 25. Final identity

The target identity is:

QORE SOVEREIGN CAPITAL INTELLIGENCE, COMPOUNDING AND CAPITAL-GROWTH OPERATING SYSTEM.

CIBO must learn how capital grows, how realized profit becomes future productive capacity, how conquered capital is protected, when to compound, when not to compound, where to compound, how fast to compound and how much marginal capital an opportunity actually deserves.
