## Why test differs
Policy rates can be observed publicly but **are not spot-based carry, forward discount, tradeable borrowing/lending or CFD swap**. Historical target definitions can change, e.g. SNB replaced 3m LIBOR target range with policy rate Jun 2019. BIS splices documented instruments, not an invariant measurement. BIS monthly data generally reflect end-of-month level **as published later**; not as-of EOM decision. Statically **lag signal by >=15 calendar days**, rank on preceding EOM before 16th. Retrospective BIS data revisions/splices cannot be assumed zero. No true vintage; conservative postmonth lag is a mitigation and makes output `RETROSPECTIVE_WITH_RELEASE_LAG_ASSUMPTION`, never PIT-VERIFIED.
Reference https://data.bis.org/topics/CBPOL, full CSV flat https://data.bis.org/static/bulk/WS_CBPOL_csv_flat.zip, WS_CBPOL `M.` ISO2 areas `AU CA CH XM/EA? GB JP NZ US`. **EUR mapping must be explicitly verified**, not invented: `XM` if BIS calls euro area `XM`, fail if absent. Metadata country instrument history preserved in audit; no manual patch of missing rate observations allowed.
## Frozen universe, signals and execution
- Exactly 28 canonical pairs all unordered combinations of 8 CCYs AUD CAD CHF EUR GBP JPY NZD USD; no aliases, no no-FX, stable alphabetic tie-break.
- EOM of preceding month BIS monthly TARGET policy rates in percent. At **first synchronized 28-market source UTC weekday on or after 16th day of each current month** require 8 prior month EOM rates from original BIS source. Signal `r_base - r_quote`, positive high-yield base long. **Long top6 / short bottom6** `+1/12` or `-1/12` notional of each pair; 16 cash. Do not invert sign ex-post. All 8 country rates required, no proxy/OECD/interbank/BIS daily missing replacement, no partial-month universe. Example for October 2017, signal **September 2017** BIS monthly values, decision after October16. 16th UTC model decision before source day's first M5 OPEN not executable; reference only.
- Entry first original M5 OPEN on/after 16th current month (all28 have eligible UTC source days >=12 M5), exit at **last native M5 CLOSE of last common eligible day before 16th of next month** (rebalance one-month hold); cash months with data absent counted 0; any era boundary or 2024-09-17 embargo crossing censored. No 00:00 price guaranteed fill, order latency/contract provenance not certified.
- For each pair (R_{spot}=S_{exit}/S_{entry}-1) dimensionless quote/base; proxy financing **annualized yield diff** `(r_base-r_quote)/100 * elapsed_calendar_days/365`. Gross MODEL = `sum(w*(R_spot + proxy_funding)) * 10000`; report separately `spot_only_bps`, `policy_accrual_proxy_bps`. Funding is NOT earned/broker swap/executable forward; this measures directional sorting plus simplified interest accrual only. **User fixed cost 2bps per side** of gross abs notional1 -> **4bps MONTHLY assumed RT cost**, additional to policy financing; NOT net broker trading.
- 28 pair graph of 8 currencies max seven independent rate diffs. Report **signed currency exposure** and concentration per month, no fit/tune hedging.
