## Literature hypothesis, no promised advantage

Zhang (2018), *Intraday patterns in foreign exchange returns and realized volatility*, Finance Research Letters 27:99–104, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2018.02.017, shows currency returns and realized volatility depend on regional trading sessions. Studies of international market opening and intraday price discovery suggest time-dependent risk/liquidity, not a proven Asia breakout strategy. **Our precise model below is a QORE hypothesis**. The rule tests whether a **completed M5 break above/below the previous Asia-time range after the London 08:00 opening** predicts a subsequent **same-day** continuation large enough to survive two assumed costs. No tradeable claim without historical BID/ASK, broker fills, contract, quote-currency normalization.
