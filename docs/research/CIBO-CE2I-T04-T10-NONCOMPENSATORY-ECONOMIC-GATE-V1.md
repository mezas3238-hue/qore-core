- maximum drawdown;
- p99 drawdown;
- peak plausible loss;
- peak margin occupancy;
- provider failure count;
- capital-conservation breach count;
- p95 recovery time;
- minimum realized capital;
- minimum liquid reserve;
- minimum optionality;
- realized net economic delta after costs.

Higher benefit cannot rescue a failed safety dimension.
