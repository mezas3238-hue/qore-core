arrive. It must learn without becoming an online outcome-aware mutator.

## Skeptic law

A report may state that a hypothesis is worth preregistering.

- select a winning counterfactual;
- promote a policy;
- rewrite history;
- mutate current productive configuration;
- claim certification.

Any future productive hypothesis must enter GEN-C14 governance as a new
preregistered candidate.
