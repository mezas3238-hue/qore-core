Market unavailable / gaps => no event, record absence.
2. **Probe**: among 12:00..12:55 bars, if max HIGH > pre-range HIGH, min LOW >= pre-range LOW, and 12:55 CLOSE is *within* [pre-range LOW, pre-range HIGH], that is `SWEEP_UP_RECLAIM`, direction **SHORT**. If min LOW < pre-range LOW, max HIGH <= pre-range HIGH, 12:55 CLOSE back within previous range, `SWEEP_DOWN_RECLAIM`, direction **LONG**. If BOTH extremes breached, neither breached or close remains outside, **no signal**. Only strict crossing, no threshold/ATR/RSI, no future confirmation. Price relative integer source, numerical scale cancels in bps; no claim of a mid-market quote.
3. **Paired equal-exposure benchmark**: `s_baseline = -sign(PROBE 12:55 close - PROBE 12:00 open)`, with equal entry/exit dates and times, designed to ask if *range liquidity rejection* adds information compared with naive contrarian of the immediate one-hour drift. A zero baseline excludes both in that day's paired sample; never discard trades based on realized return or whether signals disagree. Include zero paired-delta events. Exactly 1 event maximum per source/day.
4. **Entry**: reference `open of TARGET M5 at 13:00 UTC`; **exit** `close TARGET M5 at 14:55 UTC`. Both are OHLC references, **NOT executable BID/ASK fills**. Signed gross strategy bps `10,000*s_strategy*(exit-entry)/entry`, benchmark same with `s_baseline`, paired delta strategy − benchmark.
