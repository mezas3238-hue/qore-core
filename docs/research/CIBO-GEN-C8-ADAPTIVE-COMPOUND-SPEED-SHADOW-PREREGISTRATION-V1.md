productivity and survival-adjusted growth by changing the **pace of future
marginal capital requests** as drawdown, loss clustering, edge calibration,
Shared uncertainty, regime stability, provider condition, concentration,
margin headroom and Risk headroom change?

## 2. Control

## 3. Treatment

Treatment selects one research posture:

```text
PAUSE
DEFENSIVE
CAUTIOUS

```

The posture controls only the **maximum pace at which later marginal capital
units may be proposed for independent GEN-C4/C5/C6/Risk review**.

```text

posture -> leverage
posture -> automatic capital amount
```

Every additional unit still requires a new causal marginal-capital decision.

## 6. Non-compensatory ceiling

GEN-C8 does not average bad states away with good ones.

The treatment ceiling is determined by the most restrictive admissible causal
state.

Canonical V1 semantics:

```text
CRITICAL / UNAVAILABLE / SEVERE / HALT_NEW_CAPITAL
    -> PAUSE

RECOVERY / DEFENSIVE / DEGRADED / HIGH-UNCERTAINTY /
HIGH-CONCENTRATION / TIGHT-RISK
    -> DEFENSIVE

WATCH / ELEVATED / THIN / TIGHT-MARGIN
    -> CAUTIOUS

ALL REQUIRED STATES STABLE/HEALTHY/CALIBRATED
    -> requested research posture may pass,
       including NORMAL or ACCELERATED
```

These are policy semantics over already calibrated categories, not fitted
numeric thresholds.

High volatility alone is not an automatic rejection. When upstream calibrated
regime evidence classifies the state as WATCH rather than unsafe, V1 caps
speed at CAUTIOUS rather than PAUSE.
