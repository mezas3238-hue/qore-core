## Why this matrix exists

Run #28 completed technically but its "FULL_CIBO_COMPOUND_PORTFOLIO" treatment only wired a subset of CIBO capital science directly into the economic replay.

A true full-capability laboratory must distinguish:

- **PREDECISION / RUNTIME SHADOW** — may inspect the current causal state before an opportunity is acted on;
- **CAPITAL-ACTION / ALLOCATION** — may propose or constrain account-local capital actions but never bypass CMA or QORE Risk;
- **POST-OUTCOME SCIENCE** — may study settled results but cannot alter the already-made decision;
- **RESEARCH GOVERNANCE** — controls hypothesis testing, not trades.

"Used" therefore means the capability participated at its legal stage and emitted durable evidence. It does not mean every capability is allowed to change every order.
