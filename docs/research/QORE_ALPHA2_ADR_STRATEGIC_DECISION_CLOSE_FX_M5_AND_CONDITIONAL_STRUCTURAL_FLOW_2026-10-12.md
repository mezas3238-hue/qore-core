## 3. Do not confuse “forced fund flows” with easy arbitrage: external falsification risks

- **[Greenwood & Sammon, 2025, *The Disappearing Index Effect*](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.13410)** (published Dec 20 2024): reported mean abnormal S&P 500 addition returns declined **7.4% in 1990s to <1% last decade** despite rising passive index assets; deletions roughly 0.1% in 2010–20. A trend in nominal passive flows does not guarantee forward-looking tradable alpha.
- **[S&P DJI, 2021, *What Happened to the Index Effect?*](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/research/article/what-happened-to-the-index-effect-a-look-at-three-decades-of-sp-500-adds-and-drops/)** confirms long run structural decline and improved liquidity in major benchmark constituents.
- **[Vijh & Wang, 2022, *Negative returns on addition ...*](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/fima.12391)** finds some 2016–2020 S&P400-to-S&P500 additions had announcement returns with opposite sign to textbook older index inclusion data. Distinguish pure add/remove, migrations between indices, announced vs effective date, preannouncement prediction bias and corporate news/confounding.
- ETFs create/redeem via authorized participants and can transmit shocks; observed gross price impact is not a profit available after event publication, anticipatory trading, queueing, transaction costs and adverse selection.
- Never claim a generic "tens of bps" or model an S&P 500 index CFD as a basket of stocks with weights/events visible. Source and instrument mapping gate must succeed before any claimed beta to rebalance flows.

## 4. Conditional B feasibility dossier — assessment only, NOT PnL/test

The other architect's bounded task is an **evidence-based feasibility dossier** (no backtest, no new hypothesis, no trades, no market data purchasing without explicit owner authorization), separately reporting one preferred event-market pair such as publicly announced index constituent additions/deletions in actually accessible equities. It must answer:
1. **Legal source**: exact official issuer/index event feed, archived and immutable announcement `published_at`, `available_at`, effective-date timezone, corporate action and cancellation/amendment handling, historical constituents *as of* event date. Never infer a stock was eligible from its present membership.
2. **Economic object**: asset **stock share** vs CFD vs ETF vs futures, instrument identifier (ISIN/FIGI/exchange/symbol effective date), corporate actions, dividends, delistings, market hours, bid/ask/depth, price-volume timestamp alignment, dollar notional and broker trading rights.
3. **Causal trade opportunity**: exactly when a previously uninformed outside trader can learn the event; first reachable fill **after** publication plus a realistic latency, effect vs common factors, issuer-specific confounding and overnight gap risk. Beware prepublication event-selection and market price already adjusted by announcement.
4. **Cost model**: minimum detectable gross excess edge compatible with spread + market-impact + borrow/short + commission + FX conversion + one-sided fills, capacity. No assumption that a study's close-to-close historical BHAR is a realizable accessible fill.
5. **Statistical design**: event-day unit clustered by announcement date+issuer/index, predeclared number of competing hypotheses and comparisons, selection/delisting survivorship biases, *fresh truly out-of-time holdout*, downside tails, power requirement and null/stop conditions. No use of the current FX28 native panel as data for 2024–2026 equity events.
6. **Budget and limits**: list paid sources/providers, permitted redistribution and retention rights, API and exact executable quote venue, actual estimated cash cost; if no route to a licensed, timestamped event-and-quote series, classify `EVENT_FLOW_NOT_TESTABLE_WITH_CURRENT_ACCESS` and recommend **C continued** rather than manufacturing event surrogates.

**Pre-PnL decision gate:** `GO_TO_PREREG` only if **all** feasibility checklist items documented with verifiable original links/source identifiers, the owner has approved bounded data acquisition and the research committee decides a specific event×instrument family is economically plausible. Else `NO_GO_STAY_CLOSED`. Choosing `GO_TO_PREREG` does not authorize trades or presume an edge; the next prereg must precede any PnL inspection and include economic execution Gate UTC001.
