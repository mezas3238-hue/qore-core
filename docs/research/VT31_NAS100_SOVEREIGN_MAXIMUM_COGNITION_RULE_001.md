> **VT31 MUST USE THE MAXIMUM AVAILABLE CAPACITY OF ITS OWN INTELLIGENCE TO
> LEARN, OPERATE, AND CERTIFY. PARTIAL-COGNITION CERTIFICATION IS FORBIDDEN.**

Maximum cognition does not mean that every observed fact must force a trade.

memory, domain, causal situation update, or post-entry reassessment merely for
simplicity.

Unknown information remains explicit uncertainty. Available information must be
consumed by the synthesis.

## Entry

VT31 must use full cognition before EXECUTE / WAIT / ABSTAIN.

A shortcut entry path that bypasses the complete reasoning bundle is research
only and cannot certify.

## Post-entry

After fill, VT31 must be able to reassess the current causal market state using
the same full cognitive bundle before HOLD / TRAIL / BANK / EXTEND / EXIT when
the policy under test requires an adaptive decision.

The trader may use structure, liquidity, regime, volatility, timing, R,
MFE/MAE, target intelligence, or combinations of them. The choice must be
causal and must preserve the volume-neutral certification rule.

## R and capital separation

R is allowed as trader intelligence.

Sizing/capital engineering remains forbidden as a certification rescue.

Therefore maximum cognition may include R-based management, but it may never
turn R into an instruction to change lot size in order to improve certification
metrics.

## Certification gate

A future candidate is not eligible to freeze for certification unless it
proves:

- all three persistent memories are fingerprint-bound;
- current Situation Model is causal and fingerprint-bound;
- all required cognitive domains are represented;
- every Situation field is accounted for as actuated or explicit observation;
- full post-entry cognition is available for adaptive management;
- no terminal PnL or future outcome is used as runtime input;
- no sizing/capital engineering is used to manufacture certification.

This rule does not require every field to have an independent hard threshold.
That would encourage overfitting. It requires every available intelligence
domain to participate in the integrated cognition.
