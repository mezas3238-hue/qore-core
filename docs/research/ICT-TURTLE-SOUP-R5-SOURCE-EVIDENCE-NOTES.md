# ICT Turtle Soup R5 — source evidence notes

This note records why R5 is source-derived rather than an R4 result-derived repair.

## Standard Fractal Model versus Ideal Formation

TTrades' January 10, 2026 CISD guide describes the standard model as:

higher-timeframe Candle 2/3 closure -> drop to aligned lower timeframe -> CISD through the candles that created the high/low -> confirmed/protected swing -> continuation.

TTrades' September 12, 2026 London guide applies this directly as Daily bias -> H4 swing/Candle 2 -> M15 CISD/protected swing -> continuation.

R4 implemented that family.

TTrades' June 27, 2026 Ideal Formation guide defines a distinct, stricter formation:

- Candle 2 or Candle 3 closure simultaneously creates a protected swing;
- the confirmation candle closes in the reversal direction;
- the same closure breaks through the opposing candle series responsible for the swing;
- Ideal Candle 2 is described as the strongest version and Candle 3 is the expected expansion candle.

The associated official TTrades PDF depicts the opposing-series / protected-swing relationship on a single chart scale, with no lower-timeframe panel. Therefore R5 treats the same-timeframe opposing series as part of the authentic Ideal Formation definition.

## High-quality continuation context

TTrades' February 14, 2026 continuation-failure guide says high-quality continuation is fast, decisive, and V-shaped, while slow sideways drift before a barely-through close often represents consolidation. It also says the best continuations form within one to three candles.

These descriptions are retained as qualitative research context only. R5 does **not** invent a displacement ratio, candle-count threshold, reclaim depth, or consolidation score from them.

## Target handling

TTrades' May 23, 2026 target guide allows short-term targets and higher-timeframe runners. R5 intentionally does not add this management branch. The structural Ideal-Formation hypothesis is tested without simultaneously changing management.
