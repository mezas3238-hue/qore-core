## Adjudication of DeepSeek claims

### A. Core Turtle Soup definition

DeepSeek claim: liquidity sweep + rejection + mandatory CISD/MSS/BOS + kill-zone timing.

QORE adjudication: **PARTIAL / OVER-SPECIFIED**.

Source-backed core is stronger as:

`known/meaningful liquidity -> run/violation -> failure/rejection/reversal in contextual narrative`.

CISD, MSS, inversion, rejection block, FVG and related PD arrays can be confirmation/entry mechanisms, but direct ICT evidence recovered here does not support making `CISD OR MSS OR BOS` universally mandatory to define Turtle Soup itself.

### B. Kill Zone mandatory

DeepSeek verdict: `SESSION_DEPENDENT_SOURCE_CONFIRMED`, specifically London or NY AM kill zones.

QORE verdict: **REJECTED AS UNIVERSAL RULE**.

Primary ICT evidence includes explicit Turtle Soup examples in:

- NQ lunch macro;
- New York PM;
- broader 2024 teaching spanning London, NY AM, NY PM and Asian session.

Therefore the exact statement `Turtle Soup requires London/NY-AM Kill Zone` is contradicted by ICT's own material.

Updated source status:

`MULTI_SESSION_SOURCE_CONFIRMED`

but:

`UNRESTRICTED_24H_EQUAL_VALIDITY_UNDERDETERMINED`.

Meaning: clock time is not a universal binary London/NY-AM gate, but source evidence does not prove that every minute of the 24-hour cycle has equal validity or expectancy.

### C. All-session R2

R2 remains a valid research hypothesis, with corrected semantics:

- do not filter solely because the clock is outside London/NY-AM;
- require a causally known liquidity reference and full frozen reversal/entry mechanics;
- retain time/session as a diagnostic/context feature;
- do not claim source-confirmed equal performance across all times.

### D. CISD mandatory

DeepSeek claim: at least one of CISD/MSS/BOS is obligatory.

QORE verdict: **NOT SOURCE-CONFIRMED AS THE UNIVERSAL TURTLE-SOUP DEFINITION**.

The 2016 rejection-block teaching identifies Turtle Soup from old-high/old-low violations and rejection before any universal CISD requirement is stated. The 2024 NQ examples also use other entry arrays/confirmations.

For QORE, `CISD` may remain a deliberately frozen **candidate confirmation family**, but must be labeled QORE candidate formalization / ICT-compatible confirmation, not the only canonical definition.

### E. Sweep depth 1–2 pips/ticks

DeepSeek claim: sweep by 1–2 pips/ticks.

QORE verdict: **REJECT AS UNIVERSAL RULE / SOURCE_UNDERDETERMINED**.

No verified primary evidence recovered establishes a universal 1–2 pip/tick penetration requirement across FX, indices and metals.

### F. Wick >50% rejection

DeepSeek recommendation: wick greater than 50% of candle range.

QORE verdict: **REJECT / UNSOURCED QUANTIZATION**.

Primary ICT rejection-block material discusses wicks and body references, but no universal `wick > 50%` Turtle Soup gate was recovered.

### G. 20-bar swing highs/lows

DeepSeek recommendation: swing highs/lows defined as 20 bars.

QORE verdict: **REJECT / CONFLATION WITH CLASSIC TURTLE SOUP**.

ICT explicitly states in 2024 that he does not define his model simply by the original 20-day high/low rule. A generic 20-bar requirement must not be imported without separate source authority.

### H. HTF bias mandatory

DeepSeek claim: HTF bias is obligatory.

QORE verdict: **CONTEXT STRONGLY SOURCE-SUPPORTED; BINARY UNIVERSAL GATE UNDERDETERMINED**.

ICT repeatedly emphasizes context/narrative and 2016 rejection-block material describes ideal setups in major/intermediate trends. That supports context as important. It does not yet justify a single deterministic HTF-bias algorithm as universally canonical.

### I. Opposing liquidity target

DeepSeek claim: target opposing liquidity.

QORE verdict: **STRONGLY SOURCE-SUPPORTED IN PRINCIPLE**.

2016 material explicitly frames sell-side liquidity being run followed by repricing toward buy-side liquidity, and vice versa. Exact target-selection hierarchy among multiple pools remains underdetermined.

### J. Entry at CISD close

DeepSeek claim: canonical entry at CISD close.

QORE verdict: **NOT UNIVERSALLY SOURCE-CONFIRMED**.

Primary 2024 demonstrations use additional PD-array execution techniques. `next-bar-open after CISD` or `CISD close` may be candidate implementations, but must not be represented as the sole canonical ICT Turtle Soup entry without stronger primary evidence.

### K. Expiry at end of Kill Zone

DeepSeek claim: setup expires at end of Kill Zone.

QORE verdict: **REJECT AS UNIVERSAL RULE** because primary examples exist outside the asserted mandatory windows and no universal expiry rule was recovered.

## Engineering consequence

Do not rewrite R2 from DeepSeek's secondary rules.

Preserve R2 as a separate event-driven multi-session hypothesis with the following governance:

1. time-of-day alone cannot reject a setup;
2. source of liquidity must be known before the event;
3. sweep/failure/reversal context is mandatory;
4. the chosen confirmation family must be frozen per candidate identity;
5. CISD can be tested as one confirmation family without claiming it is the sole ICT definition;
6. no 1–2 pip universal offset;
7. no wick-percentage gate;
8. no generic 20-bar pool rule;
9. no post-result hour/pair/asset filtering;
10. session/time remains recorded for stability diagnostics.
