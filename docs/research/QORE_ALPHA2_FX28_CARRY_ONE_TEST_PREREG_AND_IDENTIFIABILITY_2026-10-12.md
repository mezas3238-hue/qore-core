## A. Literature-supported mechanism versus false data assertion

Scientific antecedents: Lustig/Roussanov/Verdelhan (2011), *Common Risk Factors in Currency Markets*, DOI 10.1093/rfs/hhr068; Menkhoff/Sarno/Schmeling/Schrimpf (2012), *Carry Trades and Global Foreign Exchange Volatility*, DOI 10.1111/j.1540-6261.2012.01728.x; BIS (2016) *Covered interest parity lost*, https://www.bis.org/publications/qr-201609/covered-interest-parity-lost-understanding-the-cross-currency-basis .

**DO NOT CLAIM** FX forward points are implicitly identifiable from a single **spot** M5 OHLC time series: spot is not a forward quote and FX returns do not uniquely identify the two underlying yield curves, forward quote, or cross-currency basis. Existing 33 archived ORIGINAL sources consumed by Alpha2 store `opened_at` plus `open_relative/high_relative/low_relative/close_relative` OHLC; source original fields read are not PIT `F/S` tenor / currency-denominated financing. Generic QORE FX forward **contract-semantic types** are not a time series of historical provider executable forward quotes. A live 2026 broker swap is not an as-of 2016 swap.

**Eligibility gate** BEFORE examining carry outcomes: fail closed unless EXACT original28 matched to complete timestamped historical **1-month FX outright forward quotes / forward points + spot pairing** for eligible monthly decision dates (entry as-of first executable signal), source identity, bid/ask or reference-mid mark, tenor/value dates and currency orientation, known/available timestamps, end 2024-09-16. A dated history of 8 actual money-market rates (not inferred from spot) is a *different, explicitly labeled policy/interest-proxy* and MUST NOT silently substitute in this prereg. No provenance => `CARRY_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_SPOT_ONLY`, **not** `CARRY_EDGE_FALSIFIED`, no PnL or surrogate. Ownership and license rights determine whether original contract data may be used, not raw OHLC alone.

## B. EXACT universe and dependence

28 FX pairs = **all 28 unordered combinations of exactly eight currencies** AUD/CAD/CHF/EUR/GBP/JPY/NZD/USD, with no duplicates, exogenous pair exclusions or non-FX. Each pair consists of a base B and quote Q, and observable forward discount signal is approximately (r_B-r_Q), specifically use **`log(S_asof/F_1m_asof)`** for canonical `BASEQUOTE` with `S=quote units per base`; high base-yield should have positive `log(S/F)`. Stable sort (descending signal, alphabetical symbol tiebreak). Long top6, short bottom6. **Six long and six short pairs are NOT 12 independent carry strategies**: differential graph for eight currencies has at most **seven** independent relative rate degrees of freedom and repeated high/low currency exposures. Report currency net-weight exposure every month; do not suppress concentrated AUD/JPY or USD factors when 6 pairs share a base.

## C. ONE fixed month-scale frozen trading reference model

- Rebalance first eligible original-source business day of each **UTC calendar month**, ranking from 1-month forward reference observations available before the decision and matching as-of spot and tenor, require exact 28 canonical pair quotes and original SHA-validated spot; **no backfill/interpolation of missing carry factor**, no retrospective name substitution. An eligible month with at least one missing original forward/spot source yields **NO 28-rank portfolio** and is recorded as a censored month; future absence cannot be used to select a subset.
- Decision PIT: `observed_at <= available_at <= decision_time`, forward and matched spot both truly observed before signal. **No same-timestamp signal & execution**; entry price reference must be measured no earlier than the first original M5 after decision+5 minutes. If available forward price cannot be paired to a tradeable entry quote and matching maturity, label reference not executable, no broker certification. All 28 sources may be stored only privately with rights; GitHub artifacts only aggregate results and SHA/eligibility.
- **Return for a long forward BASEQUOTE** at exact contractual maturity is dimensionless reference (S_T/F_{t,1m}-1), short is its negative as fixed 1-month forward quote exposure; this is NOT a CFD spot strategy's realized financing, nor a broker-USD NAV, and requires a tenor/maturity-aligned source spot at forward settlement. If F or S_T is unavailable for exact value date no trade for the whole all28 month. No 1m forward quote => NO VALID RETURN.
- Each of 12 selected positions has weight **signed 1/12 of gross initial notional** (six +1/12, six −1/12, 16 cash): total absolute notional=1, net signed pair notional=0, **currency net exposures generally nonzero**. Position closes at one-month contract expiry; each calendar month a newly established forward basket. Portfolio reference gross bps = `10000*sum(weight*(S_T/F-1))`. This compares native quote-denominated forward returns as a reference only; conversion to USD equity broker NAV remains unverified.
- **Cost assumption user-fixed 2bps per side**, on each opened and closed notional; 2bps × (entry gross notional 1 + expiry close gross notional 1) = **4 bps assumed cost per monthly basket**. Do not apply 4bps per one of 12 legs without weights, and do not present 4bps as verified broker spread; forwards are often priced via bid/ask and funding basis. Economic `reference_minus_assumed_cost` = gross−4bps, not true NET.
- DEV 2016-09-17 to **2021-12-31** inclusive, DIAG **2022-01-01** to 2024-09-16 inclusive. Month whose life crosses the split / embargo censored (never split equity after outcome). All eligible and empty months in denominators; reporting by monthly calendar, not by the 12 arms as IID. **Three-month synchronized circular block bootstrap, 2000 draws, seed 20261012** for mean monthly cost-proxy and CI95 both eras. At least **24 executed months DEV and 12 DIAG** otherwise `INCONCLUSIVE_UNDERPOWERED`.
- The only exploratory PASS requires both eras positive monthly cost-proxy mean AND **both lower CI95>0**, months sample gates and 28/28 valid as-of forward quotes for each traded month. This is an exploratory preliminary screen on exposed historical spot, **never an independently confirmed NET edge**. Otherwise fixed candidate outcome `CARRY28_PREDECLARED_GROSS_MINUS_ASSUMED_COST_FAILED` (if data / power valid) or `CARRY28_UNDERPOWERED` (if weak sample); if factor unavailable the **ONLY** valid conclusion `CARRY_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_SPOT_ONLY`.
