## Phase22 causal availability repair

A real causal defect was identified in V4 historical replay.

The frozen TRAIN prior is sourced from 2021-2022 and the Phase20 policy is
frozen in 2026. The historical replay market clock is 2015-2016. The previous
V4 code built the frozen prior with:

`as_of = market_decision_at`

which could represent 2021-2022 knowledge as if it were available in 2015.

The repair preserves two clocks:

- `market_decision_at`: historical source-state clock only
- `policy_decision_at = replay_sealed_at`: physical counterfactual policy clock

and binds:

- `train_prior_available_at = FROZEN_PHASE20_POLICY_CANDIDATE.frozen_at`
- candidate policy decision time = replay seal time
- frozen TRAIN expectation availability = actual frozen-policy time
- no prior backdating to historical market time

The sealer now emits explicit time-semantics fields and fails closed if the
prior is not available by the replay decision.
